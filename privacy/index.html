Parents and eligible students

You may request access, correction, export, deletion, restriction/pause, or raise a privacy concern. Contact the school that controls the records. Acknowledge requests within 10 school days and complete them within 30 calendar days; a shorter applicable legal deadline takes precedence. Identity is checked through the school’s existing verified family channel before records are disclosed.

School-connected family request form · Public-site privacy contact

The public-site contact can address website issues and route a request; it cannot access a school’s private database or your browser. Do not email passwords, student work, identity documents, or sensitive records. School privacy contacts must offer an equivalent assisted process for families who cannot use the app.

Age and school approval

Under 13 and unknown-age minors: school-supervised local use; connected processing requires documented school authorization for the educational purpose or verified parent authorization where applicable. Ages 13–17: the same private defaults, with applicable parent rights and service age limits. Eligible students 18 and older may exercise their own records rights. No date of birth or identity document is required for local editing.

For direct family use that requires verifiable parental consent, the operator must collect and verify a signed parental authorization through an established offline/verified communication channel before enabling covered collection. A checkbox or the Apps Script teacher code alone is not independent legal verification. No public child registration is provided here; AI connections require adult or authorized educator approval.
