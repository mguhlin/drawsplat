    Privacy and student data

    SplatWorks' published commitments limit student data to the school-authorized educational purpose: no sale, targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or AI/ML training. Local editing does not require a student account. Enabled connections, school servers, shared files, and public hosting have their own data flows.

    Read the complete security, privacy and terms policy — the authoritative policy, including data handling, retention, deletion, security, provider disclosures, and school responsibilities.

    How you use SplatWorks What the school should review
    Browser apps Work and drafts on the device; downloaded files; public-host request metadata; any optional connections you enable.
    Installed desktop apps Local work and explicitly saved files; device protections and any enabled online features. Desktop apps do not create automatic document recovery copies.
    School-hosted district suite The school's accounts, private boards, rosters, assignments, submissions, database, logs, and backups.
    Google or other connected services The approved account, permissions, selected data, provider terms, and copies kept by that service.
    SplatLMS Its separate learner accounts, learning records, PHP application, and database.

    See data and storage details · Retention and deletion schedule · Manage browser data and connections

    DPA and district agreements

    Use these documents together when preparing a Data Privacy Agreement (DPA) or National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) review:

    • District Privacy Addendum — the agreement template describing the parties, approved scope, and responsibilities.
    • NDPA / DPA review worksheet — the information needed for district exhibits: selected apps, data flows, providers, storage locations, security, retention, and contacts.
    • State & District DPA Register — agreement execution and review status. A template or pending review is not a signed agreement or district approval.
    • Privacy Notice Builder — prepare a notice for your school's actual deployment.

    For an agreement request or your district's required form, contact SplatWorks. Identify the district and selected apps or hosting arrangement; keep student records and identity documents out of the request.

    FERPA and COPPA

    For a U.S. school review, document the approved educational purpose, school control, student age groups, notices, applicable consent basis, and how families exercise their rights. The school manages its annual FERPA notice and any directory-information opt-out. App settings and published documentation do not by themselves establish legal compliance or school approval.

    FERPA/COPPA and state-law responsibilities · Parent and eligible-student rights · Consent and age

    Texas and other state reviews belong with the district's approved deployment and agreement. The same policy explains the Texas review and CIPA boundaries; app moderation tools do not replace the school's internet-safety policy or network filtering.

    GDPR and UK GDPR

    For applicable EU/UK deployments, record the controller and processor, lawful basis, notices, required agreements, processing locations, and any international-transfer safeguards. Review whether a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) or records of processing are required. Document rights requests, retention, withdrawal, and incident-notification responsibilities.

    GDPR / UK GDPR review requirements · Providers and processing locations · Rights and requests

    Public hosting uses global edge processing; the public site makes no country-only residency commitment. School hosting still requires review of enabled external services.

    Choose apps and school setup

    SplatWorks software is free. Choose the tools and hosting your school needs; installation and optional connections are separate decisions.

    • Use browser apps — start without installing desktop software.
    • Download desktop apps — Windows, Mac, and Linux downloads and installation help.
    • Download the district suite — school-hosted package and component choices.
    • Install the district suite — Docker or native installation, first administrator, backup, and recovery.
    • School setup and operations — accounts, rosters, classroom checks, and ongoing administration.
    • Google access and permissions — school sign-in, Drive access, and their different permissions.
    • SplatLMS installation — the separately installed learning management system.
    • Software licensing · Free access and support options

    Contact and family requests

    Privacy, security, agreements, and public-site questions: drawsplat@gmail.com or the contact page.

    For student records held by a school, contact that school's published privacy contact. The developer cannot retrieve private school databases, local drafts, or files on another person's device. Family access tools and the parents and students guide explain the available request paths.
