    Changes, objections and provider restrictions

    SplatWorks will give affected schools at least 30 calendar days’ notice before a material new public-service provider processes student information. Notices are published here and sent to the district notice contacts registered at drawsplat@gmail.com. Districts may object through that address, disable the affected feature, export data, or terminate the affected service before processing begins. An urgent security/legal change requires prompt notice, the reason, and the same mitigation/objection path.

    Self-hosted operators must operate the same notice process for their own changes. No optional provider may receive student content until the operator approves its contract, educational purpose, no-sale/no-profiling/no-training restrictions, security, deletion, incident notice and transfer safeguards. These are flow-down requirements for selected providers; listing a company does not represent an executed agreement.

    No student information may be sold, used for targeted advertising or cross-context profiling, or supplied for AI/ML training. Do not enable API data-sharing, feedback-training, or consumer accounts for student records. If a provider cannot meet these requirements, leave the feature off and use local processing.
