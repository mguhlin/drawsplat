Quick Start: set up your district

Start here when you unpack the complete DrawSplat + SplatWorks package. Follow this checklist, or jump to Detailed Setup. Return here whenever you update or change components.

Choose your components

Every component is checked on a new installation. Uncheck anything your district does not want to offer. Shared administration and supporting assets remain available.

Review all component checkboxes

For an existing installation, keep its current database engine. A hostname requires DNS pointing at your server and ports 80/443 available.

Follow the setup checklist

Check each task when your district has completed it. Component choices are applied by the launcher; these task checkboxes are your progress checklist.

After launching, reopen this hub at http://localhost:8080/START-HERE.html or the same path on your district’s domain.

Open selected components for verification
