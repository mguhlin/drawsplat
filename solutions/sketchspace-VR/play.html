Paper-first VR examples

SketchSpace VR is built for quick classroom play: students sketch a room, photograph the page, and explore the result in the browser.

These examples show how a flat worksheet can become a space for explanation, modeling, storytelling, and review.

MakeDraw one clear idea per wall, ceiling, or floor.
CapturePhotograph the page and crop to the outside border.
ExploreUpload, rotate, zoom, and view the room full screen.
RemixRevise labels, redraw panels, and try another concept.
