    SplatWorks ecosystem: tools, widgets, and features

    SplatWorks is the ecosystem of creative tools, classroom widgets, games, and office applications. DrawSplat is its whiteboard. Open any linked component directly, or browse Studio.

    This reference follows the same categories as Studio. Components appear in every category they support. Each entry links to the application and lists its main features. The complete suite installation includes these components; selected tools keep their own saving and integration behavior.

    Install the complete ecosystem · Download packages · Whiteboard classroom guide

    Most tools work locally in the browser. Brain Sort, Clock Wizard, Step-Splat, Toneshifter, and Vibe Check offer optional external AI connections. Editable QR codes and cloud integrations require their own configuration. See the individual application for supported formats and options.

    Choosing a keyboarding activity

    Use these activities to build keyboard familiarity, accuracy, and typing fluency. Age equivalents are approximate guidance based on the available grade bands; choose by reading ability and keyboard readiness. The games complement teacher instruction in posture, finger placement, and touch typing.

    Component Keyboarding purpose Suggested learners
    Paws & Keys Adventure Key locations and finger hints; gentle first-key practice. Ages 4–9; beginning keyboard users.
    Cipher Chase Type target words accurately while playing an arcade driving game. Grades 3–12 (about ages 8–18); bands 3–5, 6–8, 9–12.
    Wordfall Reactor Word entry, spelling, and vocabulary; rack-tile input is also available. Grades 3–12 (about ages 8–18); everyday words or grade-banded TEKS vocabulary.
    Story Sprint Copy passages with punctuation; measure typing speed and accuracy. K–12 (about ages 5–18); bands K–2, 3–5, 6–8, 9–12; custom passages.
