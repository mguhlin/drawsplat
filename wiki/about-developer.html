    About the Developer

    J. M. Guhlin is the developer and operator of DrawSplat.org and its classroom tools.

    Background in education

    Guhlin has worked in K–12 education for more than 30 years. His background includes teaching bilingual and ESL learners, leading district technology programs, and helping educators use technology. He holds a master’s degree in Bicultural/Bilingual Studies. Read more at MGuhlin.org.

    Giving back to classrooms

    DrawSplat is a way to give back to K–12 education through free, open-source tools. The focus includes multilingual classrooms, where students can show what they know through pictures, writing, and spoken explanations. Teachers can adapt the tools and activities to their learners.

    Schools can also host the software themselves. A self-hosting school operates its own service and is responsible for its accounts, student records, and privacy procedures. See Licensing and District privacy review.

    Support the project

    DrawSplat software is free. Optional contributions help support ongoing development. Support DrawSplat on Buy Me a Coffee. A contribution is not required to use the apps.

    Contact the developer

    Send a question, suggestion, or bug report to drawsplat@gmail.com. For school accounts or student records, contact the school first. Leave passwords and private student information out of your message.

    Fill in the form, then choose Open email draft. It opens your email app with a message addressed to drawsplat@gmail.com. Review it and choose Send in your email app. The form does not send a message on its own.
