    Teachers and families

    For educators

    Start teaching, plan an activity, and find the classroom and family resources for your school’s installation.

    Start with your school

    • Teacher Quick Start: open your district whiteboard and sign in with your school account.
    • Classrooms and rosters: assignments, student turn-ins, feedback, and school accounts.
    • Saving and exporting: private online boards, local files, restore points, and printable work.

    Plan a classroom activity

    • Whiteboard tools: drawing, notes, pages, and recording notes.
    • Background templates and template guidance.
    • Images, stickers, and coloring · Scratch art.
    • Graphs and picture graphs · Diagrams, maps, and word clouds.
    • Classroom widgets and tools · Collaboration and turn-ins.
    • Features and whiteboard feature reference.
    • Games, including Typing Games.

    Family access and privacy

    Use Family Access Tools for configured Apps Script requests. For district database records, follow your school’s published privacy-request procedure. Read compliance and district review and terms and privacy.

    Connections and administration

    District administrators use the installation guide and MariaDB/MySQL guide. The separate Apps Script setup supports Drive/Sheets workflows. Teacher Admin manages advanced provider settings; teachers on a configured district installation can get started with their own school account.
