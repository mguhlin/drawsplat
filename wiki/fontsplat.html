FontSplat: lettering, WordArt, and glitch text

Open FontSplat. Text and font files are processed on your device. No generation account, external font service, or text-upload backend is used.

Choose one of 16 original looks: Splat shine, Neon nights, Chrome studio, Liquid gold, Retro print, Comic pop, Rainbow ribbon, Sunset glow, Outline only, RGB glitch, Ice lettering, Ink stamp, Dimensional, Sticker cutout, Satin gradient, or Soft shadow. Change text, typeface, colors, weight, italic, letter spacing, outline, depth, arch, rotation, and slant. Use up to four text lines.

Download a PNG with a transparent or colored background at 1×, 2×, or 3×, a scalable SVG with its typeface embedded, or an editable .fontsplat.json project. SVG can optionally pulse its opacity; PNG and app insertions are still images. Raster exports are limited to 16 megapixels. Custom font files are limited to 6 MB; use fonts whose license allows embedding and sharing.

The Unicode glitch panel has separate Above, Through, and Below controls, Shuffle marks, Copy glitch text, and Copy plain text. Enable Use glitch marks in artwork to include those marks in PNG/SVG. Combining marks vary by font and reader; lower the sliders for lighter lettering. Keep a plain-text description for accessibility.

In LayoutSplat use the FontSplat · WordArt toolbar button. In WriteSplat and ImageSplat use Insert → FontSplat · WordArt. The same editor opens inside the app. Use in document inserts a PNG; it does not rewrite surrounding text. WriteSplat/LayoutSplat retain the plain text as the picture description. Save a FontSplat project to retain editable lettering controls separately.

The effects are original implementations. They do not call Cool Text or FontGlitch APIs or include those services' artwork, fonts, or code.
