    SplatWorks Wiki

    The reference for the whole SplatWorks ecosystem: standalone tools, classroom widgets, games, the office suite, and the DrawSplat whiteboard. Find component features, complete-suite installation, teaching guidance, licensing, privacy, and technical setup.

    Choose where to begin

    1. Explore tools, widgets, and features by category — Media, Play, Create, Data, Writing, Assessment, Classroom, and Organize.
    2. Install the complete district suite — Docker or native, with a rerunnable Quick Start checklist.
    3. Get started as a teacher — use your school account and open your classrooms.
    4. Review privacy and district agreements — match controls and documents to your deployment.

    Search every article using the sidebar. Existing guide links remain valid. Installation checklists also work in downloaded packages; use the online wiki when checking the current release.
