CipherSplat encrypts files, folders, and text entirely on your device.

The app has no INTERNET permission, so nothing you encrypt can leave the phone.
Your password, keys, and plaintext are processed in a local WebView and are
never uploaded or synced.

Features:

* AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with wrong-password and tamper detection
* Argon2id v1.3 password hardening (64 MiB, 3 passes, unique salt)
* File, multi-file, and text encryption; encrypted output saves to Downloads
* Optional OpenPGP public-key encryption and private-key decryption
* Integrity-check mode that authenticates a package without restoring it
* A built-in high-entropy password and passphrase generator
* No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no network access

CipherSplat is the same open-source tool published at drawsplat.org, packaged as
a self-contained Android app. Cryptography runs through the system WebView's Web
Crypto engine plus vendored, pinned Argon2 (hash-wasm) and OpenPGP.js builds.

Note: your password cannot be recovered. Store it safely and test decryption
before deleting an original.
