# For educators

Make lessons visual. Let students draw, write, record an explanation, or show what they learned. Start with a blank board, then add the tools your lesson needs.

SplatWorks includes the DrawSplat whiteboard, standalone creative and productivity tools, classroom widgets, games, and office applications. [Browse ecosystem features by category](../wiki/tools-widgets-features.html) to choose the component for your activity.

## Start a lesson

1. Open the whiteboard from your school’s link, or [open DrawSplat](../app/whiteboard.html).
2. On a school setup, choose **Options → Get Started with Your School**. Use the sign-in method your school provides.
3. Add a prompt, model one response, and give students time to try it.
4. Save a board file or export student work before you close the browser.

The [Teacher Quick Start](../guides/teacher-options-quick-start.html) walks through school sign-in and saving. A browser-only board also works without an account.

## Choose a teaching tool

| What you want to do | Features to try |
| --- | --- |
| Explain an idea | [Draw, add text and sticky notes, and organize pages](../guides/core-whiteboard-tools.html). Use audio or video notes for a spoken explanation. |
| Start with a ready-made task | [Choose a background](../pages/background-templates.html) or [use a classroom template](../guides/templates-backgrounds.html). The first board is plain white; grids are optional. |
| Support visual learning | [Add images, stickers, or coloring activities](../guides/images-stickers-coloring.html). Check each outside image’s license before sharing it. |
| Show connections | [Build diagrams, maps, or word clouds](../guides/diagrams-maps-word-clouds.html). Ask students to explain the links they made. |
| Explore data | [Make graphs or picture graphs](../guides/graphs-picture-graphs.html). Use [GridSplat](../pages/gridsplat.html) for tables, formulas, and charts. |
| Keep a lesson moving | [Use classroom widgets](../guides/classroom-widgets-tools.html), such as timers. |
| Create and practice | Try [scratch art](../guides/scratchart.html) or [classroom games](../games/index.html), including typing practice. |
| Write or present | Use [WriteSplat](../pages/writesplat.html) for writing, [ShowSplat](../pages/showsplat.html) for slides, or [ListSplat](../pages/listsplat.html) for organized records. |

## Collect work and give feedback

On a configured district setup, teachers can use [classrooms and assignments](../wiki/district-classrooms.html) to collect student turn-ins and give feedback. Your school must set up accounts and rosters first.

[Collaboration and turn-ins](../guides/collaboration-turn-ins.html) explains the available sharing paths. Features depend on the connection your school uses; district accounts and Apps Script rooms are separate systems.

## Help every learner take part

Offer a short written prompt and a model. Let students explain with text, pictures, or a voice note when it fits the learning goal. For multilingual learners, invite home-language labels alongside new vocabulary. Review outside media and embedded tools before using them with a class.

See the [accessibility statement](../legal/accessibility.html) for known limits and support needs.

## Save work with confidence

[Saving and exporting](../guides/saving-exporting.html) explains local files, online boards, and exports. Browser autosave stays in that browser and can be lost when its data is cleared. Keep a saved file for important work.
