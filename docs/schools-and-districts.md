## Privacy and student data

SplatWorks' published commitments limit student data to the school-authorized educational purpose: no sale, targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or AI/ML training. Local editing does not require a student account. Enabled connections, school servers, shared files, and public hosting have their own data flows.

**[Read the complete security, privacy and terms policy](../legal/terms-privacy.html)** — the authoritative policy, including data handling, retention, deletion, security, provider disclosures, and school responsibilities.

| How you use SplatWorks | What the school should review |
| --- | --- |
| Browser apps | Work and drafts on the device; downloaded files; public-host request metadata; any optional connections you enable. |
| Installed desktop apps | Local work and explicitly saved files; device protections and any enabled online features. Desktop apps do not create automatic document recovery copies. |
| School-hosted district suite | The school's accounts, private boards, rosters, assignments, submissions, database, logs, and backups. |
| Google or other connected services | The approved account, permissions, selected data, provider terms, and copies kept by that service. |
| SplatLMS | Its separate learner accounts, learning records, PHP application, and database. |

## DPA and district agreements

Use these documents together when preparing a Data Privacy Agreement (DPA) or National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) review:

- **[District Privacy Addendum](../legal/district-addendum.html)** — the agreement template describing the parties, approved scope, and responsibilities.
- **[NDPA / DPA review worksheet](../legal/ndpa-packet.html)** — the information needed for district exhibits: selected apps, data flows, providers, storage locations, security, retention, and contacts.
- **[State & District DPA Register](../legal/privacy-agreements.html)** — agreement execution and review status. A template or pending review is not a signed agreement or district approval.
- **[Privacy Notice Builder](../legal/privacy-builder.html)** — prepare a notice for your school's actual deployment.

For an agreement request or your district's required form, [contact SplatWorks](mailto:drawsplat@gmail.com). Identify the district and selected apps or hosting arrangement; keep student records and identity documents out of the request.

## FERPA and COPPA

For a U.S. school review, document the approved educational purpose, school control, student age groups, notices, applicable consent basis, and how families exercise their rights. The school manages its annual FERPA notice and any directory-information opt-out. App settings and published documentation do not by themselves establish legal compliance or school approval.

[FERPA/COPPA and state-law responsibilities](../legal/terms-privacy.html#regional-review) · [Parent and eligible-student rights](../legal/terms-privacy.html#parental-rights-and-access) · [Consent and age](../legal/terms-privacy.html#consent-and-age)

Texas and other state reviews belong with the district's approved deployment and agreement. The same policy explains the Texas review and CIPA boundaries; app moderation tools do not replace the school's internet-safety policy or network filtering.

## GDPR and UK GDPR

For applicable EU/UK deployments, record the controller and processor, lawful basis, notices, required agreements, processing locations, and any international-transfer safeguards. Review whether a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) or records of processing are required. Document rights requests, retention, withdrawal, and incident-notification responsibilities.

[GDPR / UK GDPR review requirements](../legal/terms-privacy.html#regional-review) · [Providers and processing locations](../legal/terms-privacy.html#service-providers) · [Rights and requests](../legal/terms-privacy.html#parental-rights-and-access)

Public hosting uses global edge processing; the public site makes no country-only residency commitment. School hosting still requires review of enabled external services.
