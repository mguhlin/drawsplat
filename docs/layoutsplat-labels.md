## Geometry and printer alignment

Geometry follows the pinned LibreOffice label database (hundredths of millimeters
converted to points), with selected US nominal sizes normalized to exact inch fractions.
Every record includes its source and product URL. Pitch, margins, dimensions, and
rows/columns remain distinct; compatible codes share their physical layout.

[Source database](https://github.com/LibreOffice/core/blob/baddf594428a2e6cb011d3e1c710f380ec02d594/extras/source/labels/labels.xml).
LibreOffice source/data licensing: MPL-2.0 / Apache-2.0. Avery product names and
numbers identify compatible paper; the templates are independent original designs.

Print a plain-paper test at actual size, with zero browser margins and no headers/footers.
File → Label sheet & printer alignment adjusts X/Y offsets and the first unused label.
Merge uses remaining slots on the first sheet and full subsequent sheets. Physical
printer accuracy is not certified by browser/PDF geometry checks. Avery 5390 is
excluded because current product dimensions differ from the older source database.
