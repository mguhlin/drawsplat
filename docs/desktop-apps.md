# Use SplatWorks on your computer

[Download desktop apps](../pages/download.html#desktop-apps) · [Use browser apps](../studio/index.html)

## Choose an app

**SplatWorks Desktop** combines PDF editing with CipherSplat file and text encryption. You can also download GIFsplat, CipherSplat, QRSplat, ImageSplat, AudioSplat or VideoSplat separately.

Office apps including ListSplat, GridSplat, WriteSplat, ShowSplat, LayoutSplat and PlanSplat open in your browser. [Browse all browser apps](../studio/index.html).

## Install and open

- **Windows:** download the Windows setup file, open it and follow the installer.
- **Mac:** download the disk image, open it and drag the app to Applications. Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Intel and Apple silicon.
- **Linux:** download the Debian package and open it with your software installer. GTK 3 and WebKitGTK 4.1 are required. Other Linux distributions can use the portable archive from “Other formats.”

These are preview builds. Windows and Mac may show publisher warnings. If your school manages the computer, ask your technology team to approve installation.

The desktop apps run locally after installation. You do not need an account, Python, Node.js or Go. Optional online features need an internet connection.

## Open a file and save your work

Use the app's Open or Choose files control. Use Save or Save As to choose where your work goes. Keep your original until you have checked the saved copy.

In CipherSplat, enter a password or import an OpenPGP key, then add your content. Files are processed and saved automatically when credentials are ready. Text results can also be copied. Keep your password or private key somewhere safe; a forgotten password cannot be recovered.

## Need help?

Open Help inside the desktop app, or [contact support](../pages/contact.html). Include the app name, your operating system, and what you were trying to do. Use a sample file when reporting a problem; keep private files and passwords to yourself.

School-wide installation and hosting instructions are in the [technology admin guide](../wiki/for-technology-admins.html).
