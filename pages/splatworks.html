SplatWorks™

Classroom office tools that live beside DrawSplat.

SplatWorks™ is the GPL-covered app family for spreadsheets, slide decks, writing, and list/database work. These tools are separated from the DrawSplat whiteboard, widgets, games, and standalone creation tools so each app can be updated and downloaded independently.
