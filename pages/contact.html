    How requests are handled

    1. If the public Apps Script endpoint is configured, your request is written to the project’s Google Sheet and an email notification is sent.
    2. If the endpoint is not configured in this browser yet, the form opens a prefilled email addressed to the DrawSplatTM project contact inbox.
    3. A real person reads it and replies at the email you provided.
    4. If you asked for a quote on Support & Professional Learning, you’ll get a scope outline plus pricing options.
