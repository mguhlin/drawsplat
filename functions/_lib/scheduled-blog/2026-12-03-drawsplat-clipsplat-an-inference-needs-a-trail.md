Let students record a brief explanation of a changing story relationship, using dialogue and an action to support what they infer.

## 1. Dialogue and action give the relationship more than one clue

A character says one thing and does something else. What might that tell us about the relationship? There's room for discussion when readers can show where their ideas came from.

Choose a short narrative with numbered paragraphs. Spend eight minutes modeling the distinction between what is stated and what is inferred. Give partners ten minutes to connect a line of dialogue with an action and explain what may be changing. Ask them to consider another interpretation. "These details suggest…" leaves appropriate room for uncertainty.

*For planning: Grade 4 ELA/Reading, 40 minutes; [Texas TEKS §110.6(b)(6)(F) and (7)(C)](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/sboe-rules-tac/sboe-tac-currently-effect/ch110a.pdf). [TCEA Essential Learning Expectations](https://mglearn.github.io/eles): S3.2 and S4.2.*

## 2. A recording lets students hear whether they explained the connection

Set aside fifteen minutes with [SplatWorks ClipSplat](https://splatworks.org/solutions/clipsplat/). Frame the text or notes rather than requiring a student's face. Rehearse what the viewer should notice, then record or import a brief clip. Trim it to the essential explanation and add a relevant title or image card.

Watch with a partner. Can the listener identify both details and their connection? Choose one unclear part to improve, export MP4, and keep the supporting notes. A teacher-provided clip works when individual recording is impractical. Students can rehearse with a sentence stem before recording or offer their explanation on paper.

## 3. Another paragraph checks the reading behind the recording

Reserve seven minutes for an individual inference about a character's feeling in a different paragraph. Require two relevant details and an explanation of how they fit.

A confident voice is pleasant to hear, but I still want to follow the evidence. If the listener can't find the connection, that's the part to work on next.
