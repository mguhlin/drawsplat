Let students annotate a paragraph's missing explanation so they can show how a revision connects a claim to its supporting detail.

## 1. A gap in the explanation gives revision a clear purpose

The writer has a claim. The writer has a detail. The reader is still wondering how one supports the other. That's a problem worth fixing together.

Prepare a short teacher-written paragraph with a missing connection, an unclear sequence, and a repeated sentence. Spend eight minutes modeling a substantive revision and explaining its benefit. Give partners ten minutes to propose two changes and read the result aloud. Focus on adding the missing explanation while preserving the message. Punctuation repairs can wait for their turn; they won't fill this gap.

*For planning: Grade 4 ELA/Reading, 40 minutes; [Texas TEKS §110.6(b)(11)(C)](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/sboe-rules-tac/sboe-tac-currently-effect/ch110a.pdf). [TCEA Essential Learning Expectations](https://mglearn.github.io/eles): S3.2 and S4.2.*

## 2. Selective annotations show exactly where the reader needs help

Allow fifteen minutes in [SplatWorks PDFSplat](https://splatworks.org/solutions/pdfsplat/) with a teacher-provided PDF of the draft. Highlight the claim and relevant detail, then add the explanation that connects them. Keep the marks selective so another reader can follow the change.

Ask a partner whether the added note answers the reader's question. Choose one part to clarify, export the annotated PDF, and keep the original. Students can rehearse the sentence aloud or use a stem before writing. Printed pages and vocabulary cards keep the task workable when devices are limited.

## 3. Another draft checks whether students can find and repair a new gap

Use the final seven minutes for each student to revise one structural problem in a fresh paragraph and explain how the change helps a reader.

If the added sentence merely repeats the claim, read it beside the detail. Ask what the reader still needs to understand. That question gives the next revision somewhere useful to go.
