Connect a mirror observation to a short written explanation so students can describe reflection in terms of the light's path.

## 1. A redirected beam gives reflection an observable meaning

A flashlight beam reaches a mirror. Where does it go? Students need a chance to investigate that before the word "reflection" does all the talking.

Spend eight minutes modeling a ray diagram and explaining its arrows. Set up teacher-guided observations with a flashlight and card openings, a mirror, a pencil in water, and contrasting materials. Keep beams away from eyes. Partners spend ten minutes connecting observations to straight-line travel, reflection, refraction, and absorption. For today's response, follow the reflected beam. An explanation that sends it through an opaque mirror needs another look.

*For planning: Grade 5 Science, 40 minutes; [Texas TEKS §112.7(b)(8)(C)](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/sboe-rules-tac/sboe-tac-currently-effect/ch112a.pdf). [TCEA Essential Learning Expectations](https://mglearn.github.io/eles): S3.2 and S4.2.*

## 2. A short document gives students room to connect the observation and claim

Give students fifteen minutes in [SplatWorks WriteSplat](https://splatworks.org/splatworks/writesplat/) with a claim, supporting details, and explanation scaffold. Draft from investigation notes, then ask a partner to underline the detail that explains the beam's change of direction.

Choose one sentence to clarify. Does it describe the light, or only name the equipment? Save the document once the writer can explain that distinction. Offer vocabulary cards and oral rehearsal; with one device, project an example and let students write their own responses on paper.

## 3. Another light diagram shows whether the explanation is usable

Use the final seven minutes for each student to identify and explain the phenomenon in a new mirror or water-glass diagram.

Ask them to talk you through the path. If they name reflection correctly but can't explain the arrows, that tells you what to model next. The saved document is a starting point for the conversation.
