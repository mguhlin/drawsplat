## 1. Looking the same does not mean containing the same amount

Two clear cups of saltwater can look much alike. Would you expect their mass readings to tell the same story? Let's give students something to investigate.

Take eight minutes to model weighing a covered container, water, and salt as one complete system before and after mixing. Prepare two equal water samples with different amounts of salt and record the amounts added. During ten minutes of partner work, compare the total readings and appearance. Keep salt, water, and containers within their measured systems. Discuss spills, evaporation, and balance precision when needed. Dissolving isn't a chemical reaction, and tasting isn't part of this investigation.

*For planning: Grade 5 Science, 40 minutes; [Texas TEKS §112.7(b)(6)(C)](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/sboe-rules-tac/sboe-tac-currently-effect/ch112a.pdf). [TCEA Essential Learning Expectations](https://mglearn.github.io/eles): S3.2 and S4.2.*

## 2. Headings help the reader follow the explanation

Allow fifteen minutes in [SplatWorks MarkdownSplat](https://splatworks.org/solutions/markdown-studio/) for a short response under Question, Evidence, and Explanation. Put the measurements under Evidence. Under Explanation, connect them to the claim that matter is conserved.

Ask a partner to point to the sentence doing that connecting work. If it's missing, there's the revision goal. Use the rendered preview to see how the response reads, then export Markdown, HTML, or DOCX. A heading scaffold, vocabulary cards, and an oral rehearsal are perfectly reasonable starting points. The same response can begin on paper.

## 3. Fresh measurements show whether the writer understands the claim

Reserve seven minutes for an individual response to a new solution data set. What supports conservation of matter? What might account for a small discrepancy?

I'd look for a student who can say what was weighed and use the actual readings. "It looks clear" belongs among the observations, but it can't do the work of the balance.
