PDFSplat OCR: review the text, protect the original

Open a scan or image in PDFSplat, choose OCR, select English or Spanish, recognize, correct the lines, and Apply. Save as PDF keeps the text selectable. The engine and both language files are bundled locally: recognition needs no document upload or external model request. Optional Drive saving remains a separate authorized action.

Editable text needs human review

Spelling, accents, layout and reading order remain approximate. Check every line against the page. Lower memory use limits batches to five pages, 500 lines and two million pixels per page; smaller images may lose detail. Start with one clear page on a modest device.

Visual covers are not redaction

Original text and images can remain recoverable under a cover. OCR and text replacement improve editing; they must not be used to conceal sensitive or FERPA-protected records before sharing. Use a district-approved real redaction tool, apply the marks, remove hidden information, save a new copy and verify it in an independent viewer. PDF passwords and image-only sanitizing are not a substitute for this review.

The OCR dialog now makes that warning prominent, and documents with visual covers keep a warning above the page. Follow the full workflow and its limits.

Tested maintenance, with remaining work visible
