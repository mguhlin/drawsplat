See your export progress in VideoSplat and MediaSplat

An animated purple bar, percentage, elapsed time, and time estimates make long subtitle burn-ins and video exports easier to follow. Your media stays on your device.

Open VideoSplatOpen MediaSplatDownload v3.1.24

MediaSplat: follow subtitle burn-in

Choose Subtitles, load a video, and import SRT/VTT or generate English captions. Start Burn subtitles to MP4 to see real encoding progress, an animated purple bar, percentage, elapsed time, and estimated remaining and total encoding time. The bar reaches 100% when the output is ready.

VideoSplat: two clearly labeled stages

Choose File → Export video. WebM shows timeline rendering progress. MP4 and OGM show rendering as step 1 of 2, followed by conversion as step 2 of 2. Each stage has its own percentage and estimate; the elapsed clock covers the entire export. The percentage starts over when conversion begins.

Estimates that adapt

After enough measured progress arrives, the apps display estimates in minutes or hours and minutes. Estimates update as processing speed changes. When progress updates stop arriving, the estimate is hidden and a waiting message appears. Animation shows that the interface is responsive; it does not guarantee the encoder is advancing.

Cancel and export again

VideoSplat now releases rendering when you cancel and stops active MP4/OGM conversion. Repeated conversions receive fresh progress updates. Export settings stay disabled during processing so the displayed stage continues to match the running job.

How long should it take?

There is no fixed number of minutes per video minute. VideoSplat renders the timeline in real time, with MP4/OGM conversion afterward. MediaSplat subtitle burn-in re-encodes the video, and its speed depends on resolution, encoding settings, your device, and other work running in the browser.

The measured estimate is: total stage time ≈ elapsed stage time ÷ completed fraction; remaining time ≈ total minus elapsed. For example, 25% after 5 minutes suggests about 20 minutes for that stage, with 15 minutes remaining if the speed holds. Early estimates can change substantially. Keep the export tab open.

Updated self-host downloads

All twelve v3.1.24 ZIPs are rebuilt with SHA-256 checksums. The independent VideoSplat and MediaSplat packages, Tools bundle, and full DrawSplat package contain the new progress interfaces. Automatic English subtitle generation, editable captions, and SRT/VTT burn-in remain included.
