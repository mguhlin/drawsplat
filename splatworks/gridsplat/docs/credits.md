# Credits and Sources

## TEKS

TEKS activity alignment is structured from Texas Education Agency TEKS source pages:

- Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills overview: https://tea.texas.gov/curriculum/teks/
- Mathematics TEKS page: https://tea.texas.gov/node/102458
- Science TEKS page: https://tea.texas.gov/educators/subject-areas/science/science

The activity data marks standards with `needsHumanReview: true` until a Texas educator verifies current official codes and wording before publication.

## Picture Graph Icons

The apple, banana, orange, and star SVG picture graph icons in `src/picturegraph/icons.ts` are original GridSplat™ project assets. They may be used with GridSplat™ under the same distribution terms as the application.

## Dependency License Posture

Last checked: 2026-06-13

GridSplat is intended to remain free for schools and self-hosters, with no per-seat software license or paid third-party package required for normal use.

Installed direct dependencies are mostly permissive (`MIT`, `ISC`, `Apache-2.0`, or `MPL-2.0`). `hyperformula@3.3.0` publishes as `GPL-3.0-only`, so GridSplat / SplatStudio is released under `GPL-3.0-only` instead of assuming a paid commercial HyperFormula license.

OAuth provider registrations for Google, Microsoft, and Dropbox are deployment setup steps, not GridSplat software licenses. They may require provider verification or district approval, but they should not create a GridSplat licensing fee for users.
